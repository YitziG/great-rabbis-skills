# Make A Lamplighter

Design help that leaves another person more able to help responsibly.

## Practice

1. **Name the light.** Define the useful knowledge, habit, access, or capability being transferred.
2. **Find the future carrier.** Choose someone who wants the capability and has the context to use it; do not assign a mission without consent.
3. **Expose the judgment.** Show not only the steps, but the cues, boundaries, and failure signals an expert uses.
4. **Create a first handoff.** Let the learner perform one bounded instance with support and a safe way to stop.
5. **Leave a small kit.** Provide the minimum checklist, example, or resource needed to repeat the work.
6. **Test independent light.** Confirm they can adapt the practice, explain its limits, and decide when not to use it.

## Capability handoff

Return:

- **Capability**
- **Willing carrier**
- **Expert judgment to reveal**
- **First supported handoff**
- **Small kit**
- **Independent-use check**

The lamplighter image belongs to the wider Chabad tradition and predates the seventh Lubavitcher Rebbe. This skill is an original enablement workflow, not an attributed quotation or mandate.
