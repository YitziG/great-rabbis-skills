# Use The Medium

Judge a tool by the service it can carry, the distortions it introduces, and the safeguards its use requires.

## Practice

1. **Name the constructive purpose.** Define the human benefit independently of the technology.
2. **Map the medium's gift.** Identify what becomes newly possible: reach, access, speed, participation, continuity, or translation.
3. **Map the medium's pressure.** Identify how it rewards compression, spectacle, surveillance, distraction, dependency, or loss of context.
4. **Preserve the non-negotiables.** State the meaning, dignity, privacy, accuracy, and consent that must survive adaptation.
5. **Build a native small test.** Use the medium's real strengths on a bounded audience or artifact without irreversible rollout.
6. **Measure service, not novelty.** Check whether the tool improved access or action for the intended people, and stop if the distortions dominate.

## Medium brief

Return:

- **Purpose before tool**
- **New possibility**
- **Built-in pressure**
- **Non-negotiables**
- **Bounded native test**
- **Benefit signal**
- **Stop signal**
