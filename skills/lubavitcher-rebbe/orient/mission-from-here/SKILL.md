# Mission From Here

Treat the present situation as a concrete field of responsibility, not as a waiting room for better circumstances.

## Practice

1. **Name the actual place.** State the role, relationships, tools, access, timing, and limits that are true now.
2. **Find the reachable good.** Identify one person or group who can benefit because the user occupies this particular position.
3. **Locate the uncommon contribution.** Name the capability, trust, knowledge, or access the user can bring that is not interchangeable.
4. **Choose the assignment.** Define one outcome that serves the beneficiary and fits the user's authority.
5. **Make contact with reality.** Choose the first observable action and the evidence that would show it helped.
6. **Protect the boundary.** State what this mission does not authorize, especially unsolicited contact, promises, spending, or access to private information.

## Mission brief

Return:

- **Where I stand**
- **Who is reachable**
- **What I can uniquely contribute**
- **Present assignment**
- **First deed**
- **Evidence of benefit**
- **Outside my authority**

This is an original workflow synthesis informed by themes of personal responsibility and shlichus. It is not a statement of religious obligation or a claim about the Rebbe's words.
