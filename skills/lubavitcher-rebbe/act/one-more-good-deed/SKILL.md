# One More Good Deed

Make one real addition without demanding total transformation first.

## Practice

1. **Name the good direction.** State what the user wants to strengthen and why it matters.
2. **Expose the false bargain.** Identify the belief that partial action is worthless unless everything changes.
3. **Choose one addition.** Select a small, positive act that is complete in itself and does not depend on a future identity claim.
4. **Fit it to today.** Attach it to a specific opportunity, place, or trigger already present.
5. **Do not inflate it.** Avoid streaks, sweeping promises, public declarations, or moral pressure unless the user asked for them.
6. **Leave the door open.** Name the next possible addition without turning it into a requirement.

## Addition card

Return:

- **Direction**
- **All-or-nothing trap**
- **One complete addition**
- **When it can happen**
- **Enough for today**
- **Possible next addition**

This is an original workflow synthesis informed by the Rebbe's practical focus on the significance of a single constructive deed. It is not a religious ruling or a judgment of the user.
