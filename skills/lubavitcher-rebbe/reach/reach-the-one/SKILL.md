# Reach The One

Begin with a person, not an audience segment.

## Practice

1. **Choose one representative person.** Use known, non-sensitive context; do not fabricate a biography or infer private traits.
2. **Name their present doorway.** Identify the need, question, place, language, or channel through which help could naturally become available.
3. **Offer one useful thing.** Make the benefit clear before asking for attention, data, commitment, or belief.
4. **Lower the threshold.** Design the smallest respectful way to accept, decline, or try the offer.
5. **Keep the person larger than the metric.** Define success in terms of benefit and agency, not only opens, clicks, or conversion.
6. **Scale only what survived contact.** Generalize after observing what helped; preserve consent and accessibility in the scaled version.

## Outreach card

Return:

- **One person in context**
- **Natural doorway**
- **Useful offer**
- **Easy yes**
- **Easy no**
- **Human success signal**
- **What may scale**

Do not send messages, enroll people, or use personal data without authority. This is an original workflow synthesis informed by the Rebbe's person-by-person approach to outreach.
