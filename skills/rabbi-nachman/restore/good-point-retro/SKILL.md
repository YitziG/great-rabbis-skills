# Good Point Retro

Do not let the repair erase the good, or the good erase the repair.

Do not automatically apply this spiritually branded frame to a personal or relationship matter. In that context, proceed only after the user explicitly chooses this skill.

## Practice

1. **Reconstruct the facts.** What was attempted, what happened, and what evidence exists?
2. **Find the good points.** Identify specific choices, capacities, protections, or moments of care worth carrying forward.
3. **Name the cost.** What friction, harm, waste, confusion, or missed signal must not be romanticized?
4. **Choose one repair.** Pick the smallest change that addresses a cause or boundary, not merely the mood after the event.
5. **Define a renewed experiment.** State the next attempt, its owner when known, and the evidence that will be reviewed.
6. **Keep unresolved questions visible.** Do not force closure where the record is thin.

## Readout

Return:

- **What happened**
- **Good points to keep**
- **Cost to face**
- **One repair**
- **Renewed experiment**
- **Evidence and review point**
- **Still unknown**

Use neutral language and observable behavior. Blameless does not mean consequence-free, and renewal does not mean repeating an unsafe experiment.
