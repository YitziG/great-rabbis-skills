# Narrow Bridge

Courage is movement with reality intact.

Do not automatically apply this spiritually branded frame outside a bounded work or project decision. For personal, relationship, spiritual, medical, legal, financial, safety-critical, or other high-stakes matters, proceed only after the user explicitly chooses this skill.

## Practice

1. **Name the crossing.** What necessary action is being avoided?
2. **Name the feared outcome.** State it concretely rather than using a label such as failure or embarrassment.
3. **Sort the fear:**
   - Actual risk that needs mitigation.
   - Discomfort that may need willingness.
   - Uncertainty that needs information or a small experiment.
4. **Choose a reversible step.** It should approach the crossing, produce evidence, and limit blast radius.
5. **Add railings.** Define prerequisites, a time or cost box, a stop condition, and any needed person, permission, or review.
6. **Set the next decision point.** Say what evidence will justify continuing, changing course, or stopping.

## Crossing card

Return:

- **The crossing**
- **What I fear**
- **Risk / discomfort / uncertainty**
- **The brave reversible step**
- **Railings**
- **Stop if**
- **Decide again when**

Do not pressure the user toward irreversible, dangerous, financially material, medically significant, legally consequential, or non-consensual action. For those, make qualified review part of the railing.
