# Simchah Check

The aim is not to feel cheerful on command. The aim is to make enough room to return to the work as a person.

## Practice

1. **Acknowledge the weight.** Name what is tiring, painful, monotonous, or discouraging.
2. **Choose one kind of honest lightness:**
   - movement or a change of physical setting;
   - music, color, beauty, or a small sensory reset;
   - a playful constraint or tiny game;
   - companionship or a brief human check-in;
   - a satisfying micro-finish;
   - genuine rest.
3. **Keep it modest.** Prefer something available now and usually under ten minutes unless the user asks for a larger reset.
4. **Protect against avoidance.** Say when and how the user will re-enter the work.
5. **Lower the emotional requirement.** Success means making space and returning with information, not forcing happiness.

## Output

Return:

- **What is heavy**
- **A fitting source of lightness**
- **Do this now**
- **Return to**
- **This worked if**

Never minimize grief, exhaustion, illness, burnout, or unsafe conditions. Sometimes the honest recommendation is rest, help, or stopping the work.
