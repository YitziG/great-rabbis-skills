# Whole From Parts

Treat each serious position as potentially carrying part of the picture, while keeping claims open to evidence and moral scrutiny.

## Practice

1. **State each position strongly.** Give every side the best concise formulation its evidence supports.
2. **Find the protected good.** Identify the value, experience, risk, or truth each side is trying not to lose.
3. **Mark the overreach.** Separate the valid part from the claim that this part explains or controls the whole.
4. **Map real incompatibilities.** Do not call factual contradiction, harm, or an unavoidable tradeoff a mere perspective difference.
5. **Build a wider frame.** Form a model in which the valid parts can inform distinct layers, times, roles, or constraints.
6. **Test the synthesis.** Ask what the wider frame predicts, decides, or changes; keep unresolved disagreement visible.

## Integration map

Return:

- **Position A / protected good / overreach**
- **Position B / protected good / overreach**
- **Hard incompatibility**
- **Wider frame**
- **Practical consequence**
- **Still unresolved**

This is an original synthesis workflow informed by Rav Kook's attention to partial perspectives within a larger whole. It is not relativism and does not require treating harmful or false claims as equally valid.
