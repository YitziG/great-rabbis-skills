# Responsible Imagination

Let imagination disclose what analysis alone cannot see, then require the vision to serve life responsibly.

## Practice

1. **Name what the current frame cannot hold.** Identify the possibility, feeling, relationship, or future that ordinary analysis keeps excluding.
2. **Open the image field.** Generate three vivid but clearly invented images, forms, or scenes that express different versions of the possibility.
3. **Read each image.** Extract the value, need, tension, and invitation carried by each; do not treat symbolism as prophecy.
4. **Apply the dignity test.** Remove any version that exploits people, glamorizes harm, violates consent, or makes private experience into spectacle.
5. **Give one image a vessel.** Translate the strongest image into a prototype, practice, artifact, or environmental change.
6. **Return to reality.** Define how the user will test whether the form enriches life rather than merely appearing profound.

## Imagination brief

Return:

- **Limit of the current frame**
- **Three original images**
- **What each reveals**
- **Dignity screen**
- **Chosen vessel**
- **Reality test**

Label all generated images and language as original. This workflow is informed by Rav Kook's serious treatment of imagination and artistic expression; it does not imitate his writing or claim sacred inspiration.
