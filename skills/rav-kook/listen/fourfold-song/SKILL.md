# Fourfold Song

Hear a decision at four scales without pretending they always harmonize easily.

## Practice

1. **Name the decision.** State the concrete choice, its time horizon, and who can actually decide it.
2. **Listen to self.** Identify effects on integrity, capacity, development, and legitimate personal needs.
3. **Listen to community.** Identify effects on the particular people, traditions, institutions, or commitments the user belongs to.
4. **Listen to humanity.** Identify broader effects on dignity, fairness, access, and people outside the immediate circle.
5. **Listen to the world.** Identify material, ecological, technical, and long-horizon consequences.
6. **Compose without erasing.** Name the strongest alignment, the real dissonance, and one choice that respects all four as far as current authority allows.

## Fourfold score

Return:

- **Decision**
- **Self**
- **Community**
- **Humanity**
- **World**
- **Alignment**
- **Unresolved dissonance**
- **Coherent next step**

This is an original decision workflow informed by Rav Kook's fourfold song of self, nation, humanity, and creation. It does not reproduce the poem or turn it into a universal ranking formula.
