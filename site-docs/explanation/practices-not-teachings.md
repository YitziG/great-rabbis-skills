# Practices, not teachings

A **practice** is a bounded agent workflow with a concrete output. It may be inspired by a theme, but its steps belong to this repository.

A **teaching** is a claim about a represented rabbi's words or thought. Teachings require reliable source work, textual context, and identified editions or translations.

Great Rabbis Skills keeps the boundary explicit:

- It never writes as Rabbi Nachman, the Lubavitcher Rebbe, Rav Kook, or another religious figure.
- It does not invent quotations, Hebrew, Yiddish, or source locations.
- Generated parables, images, and metaphors are labeled as original.
- Every installable skill names its collection and ends with an authorship or authority boundary.
- A theme associated with a wider tradition is attributed there rather than reassigned to the collection's namesake.
- Reflective practices do not replace halakhic, pastoral, therapeutic, medical, legal, or crisis support.
- Source notes invite further learning without turning a citation into authority for a modern workflow.
