# Source-theme context

This is an original modern paraphrase of themes described in the repository's source notes, not a quotation or a recovered teaching.

Rabbi Nachman of Breslov is associated in this collection with renewal, attention to concrete good points, courage, and simplicity. Renewal allows a person to return to an unfinished effort without treating prior failure as the whole truth about the person or the work. Attention to a good point concerns something real rather than praise invented to change someone's mood. The repository links the good-points theme to Likutei Moharan 282 and the courage theme to Likutei Moharan II:48. These references identify themes for study; no exact wording or edition is being quoted here.

The repository explicitly describes begin-again as an author-created workflow grouped around recurring Breslov themes. It does not claim that a particular project-management sequence was taught by Rabbi Nachman. Spiritual language grants no permission to discard obligations, erase evidence, or act on another person's behalf. Treat this context as a perspective that may be relevant to the situation, not a religious ruling.
