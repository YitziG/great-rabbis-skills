# Shared Language

This vocabulary keeps the repository concise and prevents spiritual language from becoming vague.

## Practice

A bounded agent workflow with a concrete output. A practice is inspired by a documented theme; it is not presented as a religious teaching or a simulation of its source figure.

## Teaching

A claim about a represented rabbi's actual words or thought. Teachings need reliable source work and are outside the default scope of these skills.

## Collection

A source-bounded family of practices built around one rabbinic thinker's characteristic ways of attending and acting. Collections share a quality contract but do not share a generic voice or forced taxonomy.

## Characteristic

A workflow is characteristic when its sequence and tradeoffs arise from a documented pattern in the collection's source map, rather than from renaming ordinary advice.

## Good point

Concrete evidence of capacity, care, progress, restraint, learning, or support that is already present. It is never praise invented to improve the mood.

## Renewal

Beginning the next attempt without pretending the previous attempt did not happen. Renewal carries forward evidence and learning.

## Brave step

The smallest action that meaningfully approaches the feared or avoided work while respecting safety, consent, and stop conditions.

## Honest lightness

A modest source of energy, play, beauty, music, movement, companionship, or relief that does not deny pain or evade responsibility.

## Explicit skill

A skill that runs only when the user names it. Explicit invocation is used for practices that are personal, reflective, or creatively stylized.

## Automatic skill

A skill the agent may suggest or invoke when the user's task clearly matches its narrow description. Automatic does not mean compulsory.

## Completion

A practice is complete when it returns an observable artifact and the user can decide whether to act on it.

## Practice Map

The situation-led stages within a collection. Each collection has its own stages because the thinkers organize attention differently.
_Avoid_: Curriculum, spiritual ladder, personality test, universal rabbinic method

## Help Mode

The reader's present need: Learn, Do, Understand, or Look up. Help modes follow Diataxis without replacing a collection's practice map.
_Avoid_: Skill group, journey stage

## Practice Page

The published, human-readable view of an installable practice. Its operational content is generated from the canonical `SKILL.md` source.
_Avoid_: Teaching page, devotional, course lesson
